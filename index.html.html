HisaabGo Privacy Policy

Split. Track. Settle.
Effective Date: 16 September 2026
Last Updated: 16 September 2026

1. Introduction

HisaabGo (“HisaabGo”, “we”, “our”, or “the App”) is a shared expense tracking and settlement application designed to help users create groups, record expenses, calculate balances, track settlements, generate reports, and manage shared financial records.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use HisaabGo.

Privacy contact: hisaabgo.support@gmail.com

2. Information You Enter into HisaabGo

Depending on how you use the App, you may enter member names and optional phone numbers, group names and membership, expense titles and amounts, payer information, expense splits and balances, settlement records, and other information you voluntarily enter into your expense records. This information is used to provide HisaabGo's expense tracking, calculation, reporting, and settlement functionality.

3. Local Data Storage

HisaabGo's core ledger information, including members, groups, expenses, and settlements, is stored locally on the user's device. The current version does not require a HisaabGo online account and does not operate a HisaabGo cloud server for synchronising these ledger records. Users are responsible for protecting access to their device and exported backup files.

4. Backup, Restore and Sharing

HisaabGo allows users to export and restore backup data. When you choose to export or share a backup, report, or PDF, information is transferred according to the destination or application you select through your device's sharing functionality. The selected recipient or service's privacy practices may then apply.

5. Advertising

The free version of HisaabGo may display advertising using Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google's advertising technology may automatically process information such as IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), device or advertising identifiers, app and advertisement interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. This information may be used for advertising, analytics, measurement, security, and fraud-prevention purposes.

6. Privacy Choices and Consent

Where required by applicable privacy laws, HisaabGo uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request or manage advertising-related privacy choices. Availability and content of privacy choices may vary by region and applicable law. Where required, an in-app Privacy Choices option may be provided.

7. Remove Ads Purchase

HisaabGo may offer a one-time Remove Ads purchase through Google Play. Purchases are processed by Google Play. HisaabGo does not directly receive or store your payment card or banking information. Information necessary to process, verify, restore, or manage a purchase may be handled by Google Play. After a valid Remove Ads entitlement is recognised, HisaabGo disables advertising for that entitlement.

8. Third-Party Services

HisaabGo currently uses third-party services that may process information according to their respective privacy policies, including Google Play Services / Google Play, Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), and Google Play Billing.

9. Data Sharing

HisaabGo does not sell users' locally stored expense, group, member, or settlement records. Information may be processed or shared with third-party service providers where necessary for advertising, purchase processing, privacy or consent management, or when a user explicitly chooses to export or share information. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or valid legal process.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures in the design of HisaabGo to protect information handled by the App. Core ledger information is stored locally on the user's device. Information processed by third-party services is subject to their security measures and policies. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored HisaabGo records remain on the user's device until they are deleted through the App, removed as part of the App's local data, or otherwise removed from the device. Because the current version does not require a HisaabGo cloud account and does not maintain a HisaabGo server containing users' ledger records, there is no separate HisaabGo cloud account containing those records to delete. Third-party services may retain information according to their own policies.

12. Children's Privacy

HisaabGo is a general expense-management application and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly operate a HisaabGo service intended to collect children's personal information through a HisaabGo cloud account. Advertising and third-party services remain subject to applicable Google Play policies, applicable law, and the audience settings configured for the App.

13. International Processing

Third-party providers used by HisaabGo, including Google, may process information on servers located outside the user's country of residence, subject to their privacy policies and applicable legal safeguards.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when HisaabGo's features, third-party services, legal requirements, or data-handling practices change. The revised policy will display an updated Last Updated date.
